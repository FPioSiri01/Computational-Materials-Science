1) Molecular Dynamics: Velocity Verlet Algorithm to simulate a FCC(100) Ag cluster using Lennard - Jones potential.
Implementation of: Sharp cutoff, Polynomial junction, Steepest descent procedure, PBC, Verlet cages, Andersen thermostat. 
Track the temperature and energy of the cluster and the trajectory of an adaptor on top of the cluster.

2) Monte Carlo: Kynetic Monte Carlo algorithm to simulate Ag growth on flat substrate (deposition and diffusion) at different flux rates up to a certain nominal coverage, 
which we track. 
Metropolis Monte Carlo algorithm to find the equilibrium configurations of Ag atoms on a flat substrate. 
Track the minimum energy and the chemical potential as a function of the number of atoms, 
and the mean energy and fluctuations as a function of temperature for different substrate dimensions, to se the order/disorder phase transition.
